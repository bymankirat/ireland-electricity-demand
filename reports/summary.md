**Irish Electricity Demand Forecasting (Python, pandas, scikit-learn):** automated pipeline on 2019-2026 EirGrid grid data (hourly demand, wind, CO2) and Met Éireann weather, with automatic quality checks; day-ahead forecasts accurate to 2.7% per hour on 21 months of unseen data (34% better than the industry-standard same-hour-last-week benchmark), with adaptive 80% prediction intervals (79% coverage on unseen data); quantified demand growth (19% since 2019) and the wind-carbon link for decision-makers.
